## Draft copy

> **Eyebrow:** For your team

### H2
Your team sees today's jobs — *not a group chat.*

### Intro
Providers get their own view of the same booking: today's schedule, the address, what the customer booked, and one tap to say they're on the way. No forwarding screenshots, no "what's the gate code again."

### Three phone-frame blocks

**Today's schedule.**
Each provider sees only their assigned jobs, in order, with arrival windows and travel time between stops.
*Alt:* "neatr provider app showing a cleaner's schedule of assigned jobs for today"

**On my way — sent for them.**
One tap notifies the customer by SMS. The dispatch board updates at the same moment, so the office never has to ask "have you left yet?"
*Alt:* "neatr provider app sending an on-my-way text to a customer"

**Proof of work, built in.**
Before/After photos per space, attached to the job record. Disputes end before they start.
*Alt:* "neatr provider app capturing before and after photos of a cleaned room"

### Closing line
Owner admin, provider app, customer portal — three views of the same booking, each showing exactly its slice.
