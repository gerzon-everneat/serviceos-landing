# 08 — Resources / blog teaser

**Placement:** after the founder note, before the closing CTA. **Do not ship until ≥3 real articles exist** — an empty blog hurts more than no blog.
**Section id:** `#resources`
**Purpose:** the long-term SEO engine. The landing page can't rank for informational queries; articles can, and they funnel internal links back here.

---

## Article guidelines (for whoever writes them)

- Written from Everneat operating experience — first-hand numbers beat generic listicles and are the E-E-A-T moat.
- Each article internally links to the landing section it supports (pricing article → `#product` pricing-matrix block; no-show article → card-on-file block; switch article → `#switch`).
- `Article` JSON-LD with a real `author` Person on every post; publish dates honest, updates dated.
- 1,200–1,800 words; one worked example or real number per article minimum.

---

## SEO notes

- These three target queries are informational, high-volume-for-the-niche, and map directly to product strengths — the funnel is query → article → landing section → signup.
- The landing-page section itself carries no keyword weight; its job is internal linking and freshness signals.
- Cadence beats volume: one genuinely good article a month outranks ten thin ones in this niche.
