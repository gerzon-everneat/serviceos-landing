### H3 — An online booking page your customers actually finish
Your customers pick a service, answer a few questions about their home, choose a two-hour arrival window, and save a card — as guests, no account required. The whole flow runs about a minute; the recording above shows one finish in 1:07. Every quote is computed live from your pricing matrix, so nobody waits for a callback to hear a price.

### H3 — Scheduling and dispatch that check availability for you
Every booking lands on a dispatch board. When you assign a provider, neatr has already checked their working hours, existing jobs, and travel buffers — you only see people who can actually make it. Reassignments swap cleanly, and the customer's chat thread follows the job.

*Screenshot:* dispatch board with a booking being assigned.

### H3 — Automated appointment reminders by email and SMS
Confirmations, reminders, and on-my-way messages send themselves. You control every notification from one settings screen — turn any of them off, and neatr keeps an audit trail of what went out.

### H3 — Card on file: verified at booking, charged after the job
Customers see **"No payment due today"** at checkout. The card is verified and saved — you charge when the job is done. No prepayment friction for them, no no-show losses for you.
