# 03 — Social proof / testimonials

**Placement:** between Product and Pricing (pre-pricing trust position).
**Section id:** `#proof`
**Purpose:** conversion + E-E-A-T. Not a keyword section.

---

## Draft structure (copy pending real quotes)

> **Eyebrow:** From the field

### H2
Owners who stopped taking bookings *by text.*

### Quote cards (2–3)

```
"<verbatim quote about a concrete before/after — e.g. double-bookings,
 phone-tag, no-shows>"
— <Full name>, <Business name>, <City, ST>
```

Each card: quote, name, business, city. Photo or business logo if permitted. Nothing anonymous — "Sarah M." reads as fabricated.

### Fallback (ship this until real quotes exist)
Do **not** ship placeholder or invented testimonials. Until 2–3 real quotes are collected, this slot is filled by the Founder note (see [07-founder-note.md](07-founder-note.md)) moved up the page.

---

## How to source the quotes

1. Early-access tenants who completed ≥10 bookings — ask one question: *"What did booking look like before neatr?"*
2. Get written permission for name + business + city.
3. Keep the verbatim wording, typos lightly cleaned. Specific beats polished.

---

## SEO notes

- **Hard rule:** no `Review` or `AggregateRating` JSON-LD for testimonials hosted on your own site — Google's guidelines treat self-serving review markup as spam; it earns no rich result and risks a manual action. Plain HTML quotes only.
- Real names + businesses are themselves E-E-A-T signals and occasionally rank for `"<business name>" + neatr` navigational queries — fine, free.
